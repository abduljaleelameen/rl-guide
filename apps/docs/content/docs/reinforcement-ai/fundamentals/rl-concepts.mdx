## Single-Turn vs Multi-Turn RL

In traditional RL (like games), actions are quick and frequent. In LLM RL:

- **Single-turn:** One complete response = one "action." Reward given after the full response.
- **Multi-turn:** Each message in a conversation could be an action. More complex but enables better dialogue skills.

<Callout>
  Most modern LLM RL implementations use single-turn for simplicity and faster training. Multi-turn is explored in research for conversational agents.
</Callout>
